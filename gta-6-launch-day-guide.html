GTA 6 launch day guide: release date, platforms, pre-load and download size

GTA VI arrives November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Here's when you can pre-load, how much space to clear, and what to sort out before launch night.

By The Leonida Herald, November 17, 2026

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026, after two delays that pushed it back from fall 2025 and then from May 2026. It's coming to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PS4 or Xbox One version, and Rockstar hasn't announced a PC release.

Pre-loading

If you bought the game digitally, pre-loading has been open since November 10, so you can download it now and play the moment it unlocks. Boxed copies work differently: the box holds a download code rather than a disc, and those codes became available from November 12.

Clear some space

The download is large, up to 212 GB depending on your console. That's a big chunk of a standard PS5 or Series X drive, so check your free space before launch night rather than during it. An approved expansion drive is worth considering if you're short.

The pre-order bonus

Anyone who buys before November 20 gets the Vintage Vice City Pack, which includes a classic '55 Vapid Stanier sedan, a garage, outfits for both leads and a weapon pattern. Digital buyers also get a free month of GTA+. See our editions guide for the full breakdown.
