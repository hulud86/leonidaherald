Vice City

Leonida's neon-lit centerpiece, with art deco hotels on Ocean Beach, the busy streets of Little Cuba and a cruise port that calls itself the capital of the world.

By The Leonida Herald, November 17, 2026

Vice City returns as the heart of GTA VI. It's a modern take on the series' classic Miami-style city, swapping the 1980s for the present day while keeping the sun, the neon and the excess.

Places to know

  • Ocean Beach, with white sand and pastel art deco hotels
  • Little Cuba, a compact, busy neighborhood of narrow streets
  • VC Port, which bills itself as the cruise ship capital of the world

Getting around

Raised freeways and crowded streets make the city slower to cross than the open countryside. Previews suggest that breaking line of sight will matter more than raw speed when you're trying to lose the police here.
