GTA 6 Coming Home walkthrough: Lucia's release, the errands and the night at the Rusty Anchor

Jason picks Lucia up from prison and takes her out to the Rusty Anchor with their friends. The optional errands beforehand set where your relationship starts.

By The Leonida Herald, November 25, 2026

Heads up: some details on this page aren't confirmed yet. We say so where that's the case and will update the page as we check them.

Coming Home is the payoff for everything Jason has been doing since the Prologue: Lucia gets out. It is a long, mostly relaxed mission with a short fistfight near the end. You can't fail it in any of the ways guides describe.

Before you go: the homecoming errands

After Feng Shui, a set of small errands opens up to get things ready for Lucia. Two of the three guides we checked call them optional; the third says they can be skipped. They are worth doing, because they lift Lucia's opinion of Jason from the start.

  • Buy her a necklace at the jewelry shop. Two guides say it comes up again in Chapter 2, so don't skip this one.
  • Pick up groceries. Grab a few items before the game tells you to leave.
  • Buy flowers.
  • Collect Lucia's clothes from her friend Andy at a bar, and keep them in the car's trunk.
  • Get Jason a haircut at the barber.

Two guides say doing all five starts the relationship at three of four hearts; you can check it in the Jason and Lucia menu. The same two describe optional repairs around Jason's house (a bedroom wall, the stairs, the roof and the balcony railing), which earn a reaction from Lucia later on.

How to start

The marker sits on the prison, but two guides say you first need to sleep at home until Lucia calls. If the call doesn't come, sleep again or drive around for a while. One guide doesn't mention this step at all, so your game may let you go straight there.

Objectives

  • Drive to Hamlet Correctional and speak to the guard at reception. A flashback plays.
  • Meet Lucia at the gate and walk her to the car.
  • Drive to the Rusty Anchor in north Key Lento. Order drinks at the bar (two guides put it at $6 for two beers), and if you brought her clothes, Lucia changes in the bathroom.
  • Find Lucia out behind the bar and talk.
  • Mingle. Cal, Donnie and others are around, with a few optional chats. Guides agree none of them change anything.
  • Dance with Lucia when prompted. One guide treats this as a choice you can turn down; accepting gives a small relationship bump.
  • Find Mattia, who is on the phone behind the bar, and bring him a beer. He then challenges Jason to pool.
  • Play a few shots until a group from the bar starts trouble and Jason takes it outside.
  • Win a short fistfight. Watch the other man's shoulders: block when he winds up, then hit back.
  • When the police turn up, run with Lucia and follow her until the closing scene.

Tips

  • The fight is the first real melee tutorial. Blocking on the wind-up is far more reliable than trading punches.
  • One guide says Rhonda's pet lizard escapes the bar during the night; focus mode will show it in a bush across the street. This isn't confirmed elsewhere.
  • One guide says that washing Jason's hands at the bar sink gives a short Focus boost. Also not confirmed elsewhere.

What happens (spoilers)

Story spoiler: tap to reveal

Kat, Lucia's friend from prison, turns up pregnant, and Jason gets to feel the baby kick.

The night ends with the police arriving and Jason and Lucia slipping away together to the beach, then back to Jason's place, where Lucia stays.

The next morning Donnie calls with a job, which leads into Clean Up After Donnie.

Next

Clean Up After Donnie follows. One guide adds that from here you can switch between Jason and Lucia; that isn't confirmed elsewhere yet.

A note on order

Guides number the early Chapter 1 jobs differently. We follow GTABase's chronological list, which puts Coming Home eleventh.
