GTA 6 Feng Shui walkthrough: rearranging Jason's house with Cal

Cal decides Jason's place isn't fit for Lucia's homecoming, so the two of them move the furniture and play video games. No combat, no way to fail.

By The Leonida Herald, November 23, 2026

Heads up: some details on this page aren't confirmed yet. We say so where that's the case and will update the page as we check them.

Feng Shui is one of the quietest missions in the game: an evening in with Cal. It's mandatory, short and a good break after Hey Arnold!

How to start

Cal texts Jason and a marker appears on his house. Walk in to begin.

Objectives

  • Grab a beer from the fridge and sit on the couch with Cal. He points out that the place needs work before Lucia gets home.
  • Move the furniture as prompted: the TV over by the door, the coffee table in front of it, then the couch (talk to Cal until he asks you to move it) and finally the chair from under the lamp.
  • Sit down and play the JoyStation console with Cal. The first game is a puzzle game called Tenebrae Lux; after a little while Jason asks to swap.
  • The second game is Street Trash III, a top-down shooter. You can't win it. Play until it ends or your character dies, and the closing scene starts.
  • The mission wraps up the next morning.

Tips

  • Cal fusses over where the coffee table goes. You can accept it or keep adjusting; guides agree it doesn't matter.
  • In Tenebrae Lux, spiked and triple pieces knock out flat ones, according to one guide.
  • The JoyStation stays in the house, so you can play it whenever you're back.

What happens (spoilers)

Story spoiler: tap to reveal

The next morning brings the news Jason has been waiting for: Lucia is being released. Guides differ on whether the call comes from the prison or from Lucia herself.

Next

Coming Home, where Jason goes to meet Lucia, follows. Before that, a set of optional errands to get the house ready for her opens up.

A note on order

Guides number the early Chapter 1 jobs differently because several open up at the same time. We follow the order most guides agree on; if your game offers them in a different order, nothing breaks.
