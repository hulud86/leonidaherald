GTA 6 Gone Fishing walkthrough: Jason's first job

Jason and Cal pick up a drug drop off the Keys, and a simple job turns into a stealth rescue and a seaplane escape. It also gets you into Jason's house.

By The Leonida Herald, November 19, 2026

Heads up: some details on this page aren't confirmed yet. We say so where that's the case and will update the page as we check them.

Gone Fishing is Jason's introduction. He and Cal are out on Cal's boat waiting for a pilot, Donnie, to drop a shipment into the water.

Objectives

  • Take the wheel when Cal asks and follow Donnie's plane. Steer to each floating package and slow down early so Cal can hook it. There are three. If you lose sight of one, use Focus (both sticks) to find it again.
  • Follow the marker to the meeting point and use the zoom to watch Donnie's handover on the dock.
Story spoiler: tap to reveal

The deal goes wrong and Donnie is grabbed by a local gang, the Kingfishers. You need to get him back.

  • Bring the boat in quietly and go ashore at the camp, an RV park in the wetlands.
  • Sneak through. Crouch, approach guards from behind, grab and subdue them. Tall grass on the edge of the camp gives cover, and you don't need the rifle lying around.
  • Find the shack marked on your map, go in and reach Donnie. One guard will rush you; beat him unarmed to keep things quiet, then grab the health item (Zombix) in the shack.
  • Optional: a dollar marker leads to a trailer with cash in it. Someone jumps you with a knife on the way in, so be ready for the prompt.
  • Get to Donnie's floatplane on the dock and take off over open water. Don't turn back toward the camp if you're taking fire.
  • Fly to Brian's Marina. Slow your descent and land on the water short of the dock, then taxi in.
  • After the cutscene, drive Cal home, then drive to Jason's house and go up the outside stairs to finish.

Tips

  • Jason starts with almost nothing, so a fight in the open is hard. Knockouts beat gunfire here. There's an optional hammer behind one building if you want a melee weapon.
  • The landing is on water, not a runway. Come in slowly and level.
  • The trailer cash is worth the detour early in the game, when money is tight.

Rewards

Finishing the mission opens Jason's house. You can change clothes and get drinks there, and one guide reports you can work on renovating it later, which is said to earn Focus points and improve things with Lucia; that last part isn't confirmed by other guides yet.

What happens next (spoilers)

Story spoiler: tap to reveal

You meet Brian Heder, the smuggler Donnie flies for and Jason's landlord, at his marina.

Lucia gets in touch to say she's being released. Guides differ on whether it's a phone call or a face-to-face talk.

Next

Gear Up, where Jason buys a long gun, is the next story mission.
