GTA 6 Prologue walkthrough: Lucia's first mission

The game opens with Lucia behind bars and a flashback to the job that put her there. It's short, mostly scripted and can't be failed in any meaningful way.

By The Leonida Herald, November 19, 2026

The Prologue starts as soon as you begin a new game. You play as Lucia, who is serving time. Guides name the jail as Hamlet Prison; Rockstar's pre-launch profile called it Leonida Penitentiary, so the in-game name may differ from the marketing.

Objectives

  • Talk to your cellmates. Holding the aim button brings up conversation options, so you can be friendly or pick a fight. It doesn't change the outcome.
  • Follow the guard, CO Wilson, down the corridor. He pauses for a phone call, then takes you to a counselor's office. You can chat to other inmates on the way.
  • The counselor's questions trigger a flashback: Lucia robbing a payday loan store. Aim at the teller, threaten them and scoop up the cash. A customer tries to tackle you; fight them off.
  • Lose the cops. You run across rooftops and through a mechanic's shop, jumping and climbing as prompted.
  • Return to the coffee shop marked on your map.

Tips

  • You can't actually escape the chase. Two of the three guides we checked say the game cuts back to the counselor's office whatever you do; one describes losing the police and reaching the coffee shop. Either way the result is the same, so don't waste time trying to outrun the script.
  • The arrest at the end can't be avoided. It's a story beat, not a failure.
  • This is a good moment to learn the basics: the aim-to-talk system, melee and parkour controls all come back later.

What happens (spoilers)

Story spoiler: tap to reveal

The coffee shop matters because Lucia left her young son, Tommy, there while she did the robbery. Staff had already called the authorities.

Tommy is taken into child welfare and Lucia is arrested. Getting out of prison and back to him becomes her driving goal.

Next

The story then switches to Jason for Gone Fishing, the second mission. Lucia returns as a playable character later in Chapter 1.
