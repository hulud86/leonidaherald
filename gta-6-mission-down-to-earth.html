GTA 6 Down to Earth walkthrough: Brian's seaplane run to Bobcat Bay

Brian Heder puts Jason and Lucia in a seaplane for a cocaine pickup and three drops. Your first real flying mission, with a soft water landing to get right and a surprise waiting at home.

By The Leonida Herald, November 27, 2026

Heads up: some details on this page aren't confirmed yet. We say so where that's the case and will update the page as we check them.

Down to Earth is the first job that puts you in the air. Most of it is flying a small seaplane between drop points; the one real danger is landing on the water too hard.

How to start

Finish Buck Me and Clean Up After Donnie. Beebom says that after Jason spends the night with Lucia, Brian texts him the next morning, and a Meet Brian marker appears at the marina southwest of Jason's house in Key Lento.

Objectives

  • Meet Brian at the marina and follow him to his boat.
  • Clean the boat with the pressure washer. Keep going through the short scene where Lucia arrives, until Brian hands you the errand.
  • Board Brian's seaplane with Lucia and fly to Bobcat Bay, following the marker.
  • Land near the marked boat and collect the package.
  • The handover turns into a shootout. Deal with the gunmen until Lucia is back aboard.
  • Make three drops. Both detailed guides have you land on the water for the early ones; for the last, fly low alongside a moving boat so Lucia can hand it over.
  • Fly back and land at the dock by Jason's house.
  • Hide the leftover drugs in the washing machine in the shed, then join Lucia to finish the mission.

Flying tips

  • Ease down onto the water. Both guides warn that hitting it hard destroys the plane and fails the mission.
  • Beebom notes the flight controls are inverted by default: pull back to climb, push forward to descend. Check your settings before take-off if that feels wrong.
  • Once you're on the water you can taxi the plane like a boat for short distances, which helps line up with a marker.

Choices

Beebom says the way you handle the gunmen at the pickup feeds Jason's Criminal Profile, and that killing or downing them with a gun moves him toward a harder rank. We haven't seen a second guide confirm this, and we don't know what the profile changes later.

Rewards and unlocks

Beebom reports $500 paid into BuckMe and the Focus ability unlocked for both Jason and Lucia. VGC doesn't mention either, so treat both as not yet confirmed. Beebom also flags a missable Meditation Spot that opens straight after this job.

What happens (spoilers)

Story spoiler: tap to reveal

As you land back home, Lucia's parole officer turns up unannounced, which is why the drugs have to vanish into the washing machine.

Lucia talks the officer round about where she has been, with Jason backing her up, and the visit passes.

Next

Day Release and Clear Skies both open after this job, and you can play them in either order. Swamped follows once both are done.

A note on order

Guides number these jobs differently: Beebom calls Down to Earth the 13th mission, VGC and Game Rant the 14th. We keep following GTABase's chronological order.
