GTA 6 Clear Skies walkthrough: escorting Pilot Pete and the airstrip deal

Brian sends Jason to babysit Donnie on a smuggling flight. The plane work is easy; the deal at the second airstrip is where it goes wrong.

By The Leonida Herald, November 27, 2026

Heads up: some details on this page aren't confirmed yet. We say so where that's the case and will update the page as we check them.

Clear Skies is the second flying job and the first proper firefight against a biker gang. Bring a shotgun if you have one, or pick one up mid-mission.

How to start

It opens after Down to Earth. Head to Southwind, where Donnie is working on the seaplane at the boatworks; he sends you to Brian at the tiki bar nearby.

Objectives

  • Talk to Brian, then go back to the plane.
  • Taxi off the slipway into the water and take off. GTABase says you can try to lift off from the road, but it's riskier.
  • Fly to the intercept point and catch up with Pilot Pete's plane. Stay right above him: the idea is that air traffic control sees one aircraft, not two.
  • Escort Pete to the first airstrip.
  • Fly on to the next airstrip, land carefully and taxi into place.
  • Follow Donnie on foot to the deal. It goes bad when bikers from the Final Chapter turn up.
  • Fight from cover, then clear the building by the airstrip, and get Donnie out of it.
  • Fight your way out and hold off the bikers while Donnie gets to the plane.
  • Take one of the bikers' motorcycles and chase after him to the meeting point, then board the plane.
  • Back home, a short scene with Lucia ends the mission.

Tips

  • Kill the gunmen around Donnie before you reach him; guides warn the mission fails if he dies.
  • GTABase says you can take a shotgun from one of the fallen bikers if you don't have one yet.
  • The bikers leave several motorcycles at the airstrip. GTABase notes you can scan them with the WAiNK app.
  • Watch for enemies coming round the sides of the airstrip buildings; VGC says the flankers are the main threat.

Not yet confirmed

The three guides we read place the airstrips and the final meeting point in different spots, so we've left names out. GTABase lists a $1,400 payout; the others don't give one. GTABase also names a job called Building a Life right after this one, which the mission lists we checked don't include. We'll update this when more guides agree.

What happens (spoilers)

Story spoiler: tap to reveal

The deal is a setup. The Final Chapter bikers are after Donnie personally, not the product, and they grab him as soon as the shooting starts.

Jason finds him tied to a chair being questioned, and has to cut him loose and get him out.

Next

Day Release, if you haven't played it. Swamped opens once both are done.
