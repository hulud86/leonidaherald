Crime Beat

Mission walkthroughs for every story job, in order, with objectives, tips and rewards.

Story missions in the order you play them. Each page lists objectives, tips and rewards, with story spoilers hidden until you tap. We add missions as we confirm them.

  • GTA 6 Prologue walkthrough: Lucia's first missionThe game opens with Lucia behind bars and a flashback to the job that put her there. It's short, mostly scripted and can't be failed in any meaningful way.
  • GTA 6 Gone Fishing walkthrough: Jason's first jobJason and Cal pick up a drug drop off the Keys, and a simple job turns into a stealth rescue and a seaplane escape. It also gets you into Jason's house.
  • GTA 6 Gear Up walkthrough: buying Jason's first long gunA quick trip to Ammu-Nation for a shotgun or rifle, then a scrap with three Kingfishers in the car park. Fight them with your fists and you'll keep the police out of it.
  • GTA 6 Unwanted Attention walkthrough: the impound lot jobCal takes Jason to Hamlet to lift a red Phoenix out of a police impound. You'll pick up the police scanner, learn how heat works and earn $150.
  • GTA 6 Lab Fees walkthrough: collecting Brian's rent (door codes)Brian sends Jason to collect rent from a gift shop in the Keys. The tenant can't pay, so you take something else instead. Door codes: 0420 for the back room, 7700 for Brian's lockup.
  • GTA 6 Just a Scratch walkthrough: free repair at Coastal Auto CareA short, easy job: take Jason's battered '70 Ganado to Joey at Coastal Auto Care for a free repair and respray. It introduces the game's mod shops.
  • GTA 6 Pipework walkthrough: Mandy's rent and the blocked sinkBrian sends Jason to collect rent from a tenant named Mandy, who won't pay until her kitchen sink is fixed. A plunger, a wrench and a free cigar later, the job's done.
  • GTA 6 Hey Arnold! walkthrough: should you kill or spare Arnold?Brian, Cal and Jason go looking for a man who has stopped answering Brian's calls. It ends in a car chase, a foot chase and the game's first big choice. Our advice: spare him.
  • GTA 6 Island Dreams walkthrough: Kenton's dive, the wreck and the sharkJason takes a bachelor party scuba diving for Kenton, a dive-shop owner in the Keys, and finds treasure on a shipwreck. It pays $400 and opens up underwater treasure hunting.
  • GTA 6 Feng Shui walkthrough: rearranging Jason's house with CalCal decides Jason's place isn't fit for Lucia's homecoming, so the two of them move the furniture and play video games. No combat, no way to fail.
  • GTA 6 Coming Home walkthrough: Lucia's release, the errands and the night at the Rusty AnchorJason picks Lucia up from prison and takes her out to the Rusty Anchor with their friends. The optional errands beforehand set where your relationship starts.
  • GTA 6 Clean Up After Donnie walkthrough: recovering the lost packageDonnie has lost a drop in the swamps and Jason has to get it back from the Kingfishers. A short boat job that opens up selling to Donnie at his trailer.
  • GTA 6 Buck Me walkthrough: the bank, the loan and the BuckMe appJason and Lucia try to open a bank account in Key Lento and end up with the BuckMe app instead. A short story mission that introduces the game's banking.
