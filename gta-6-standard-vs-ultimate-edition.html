GTA 6 Standard vs Ultimate Edition: what the extra $20 gets you

The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99. Both play the same story. Here's what the upgrade adds and who it's worth it for.

By The Leonida Herald, November 17, 2026

Rockstar is selling two versions of GTA VI. Both contain the full game and the same story. The difference is a bundle of extra single-player items that come with the Ultimate Edition.

What every pre-order gets

The Vintage Vice City Pack comes with any copy bought before November 20, Standard or Ultimate:

  • A '55 Vapid Stanier sedan and a Shore Court garage with a weapon locker and a drop-off point for fencing goods
  • Retro outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia
  • A tropical palm-tree pattern for most weapons
  • One free month of GTA+ on digital pre-orders

What the Ultimate Edition adds

  • Extra vehicles, including a '95 Grotti Cheetah, a '67 Vapid Dominator buggy with its own garage, a Shitzu Squalo boat, a Dinka Enduro bike and a kayak
  • Extra revolvers and personalized versions of two pistols
  • Vice City-style outfits and tattoos
  • Two extra pieces of content: a raid on a PTT Youngin$ compound and a classic-car job for a character named Wyman
  • Access to five shops: Rideout Customs, Sara's Unisex Salon, Stock 305, Electric Fang Tattoo and One-Eyed Willie's

Our verdict

For most players, the Standard Edition is enough. The Ultimate items give you a head start on cars, guns and style, but none of it changes the story. The Ultimate Edition is digital only, so if you want a box on the shelf, Standard is your only choice. And there's no rush: Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade later if they decide they want it.
