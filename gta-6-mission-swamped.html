GTA 6 Swamped walkthrough: the Kingfishers camp and the airboat chase

Brian takes Jason to a meeting with the Kingfishers out in the swamps. It's the biggest fight of Chapter 1 so far, ending in a helicopter and airboat chase. Spoilers are hidden.

By The Leonida Herald, November 27, 2026

Heads up: some details on this page aren't confirmed yet. We say so where that's the case and will update the page as we check them.

Swamped turns the Heder side of the story serious. Expect a long gunfight on foot, a stretch in a helicopter and a boat chase to finish. Stock up on ammo and armor first.

Before you go

  • Finish any of Donnie's optional jobs and sell any product you're holding first. A wiki guide warns some of Donnie's side content can't be done after this point.
  • Bring a rifle and grenades if you have them.

How to start

Finish Day Release and Clear Skies, then meet Brian in the back room at his marina in Key Lento. VGC puts the start at the bar instead, so check both if the marker isn't obvious.

Objectives

  • Get in Brian's truck and drive to the Kingfishers camp in northwest Mariana County.
  • Walk through the camp with Brian to the pig pen at the far end and inspect it.
  • The meeting falls apart. Fight your way out with Brian, using cover; you don't have to kill everyone, but it's hard to get out without thinning them.
  • Clear the gunmen around the helicopter at the edge of the camp, then board it.
  • From the air, chase the Kingfishers fleeing the camp. Shoot from the helicopter and destroy their truck when prompted.
  • Jason drops into an airboat. Pull alongside the boat you're chasing and hold the jump button to cross over, then finish the last man with the on-screen prompt.

Tips

  • The mission fails if the Kingfishers get away or you hit the wrong person, so pick single shots from the helicopter rather than spraying. Focus mode helps.
  • A wiki guide says a shotgun is lying by some crates on the road out of the camp, and that a gunman hides behind the far float near the helicopter.

Choice afterwards

Back home, a timed prompt lets Jason tell Lucia about something Brian said. GTABase and a wiki guide both say taking it raises the relationship and letting it pass gives nothing; the wiki adds it costs you a relationship level at the end of Recalibration. That second part isn't confirmed elsewhere.

Not yet confirmed

VGC says taking out the last Kingfisher from the helicopter can end the chase early; the other two guides always have you finish it from the airboat. GTABase and the wiki both give $1,000, though the wiki says some players got no payout.

What happens (spoilers)

Story spoiler: tap to reveal

The meeting is an ambush. Donnie is dead, and the pig pen is where Brian and Jason find what's left of him.

The Kingfishers grab Lori, Brian's wife, during the escape, which is what the chase is about.

Afterwards Brian says he's done with the drug business now that Donnie is gone, and Lori backs him. That retirement is the news Jason can share with Lucia.

Next

Don Gone, which starts with a text from Brian about clearing out Donnie's trailer.
