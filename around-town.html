Around Town

Leonida's regions, plus side quests and things to do in each one.

  • Vice CityLeonida's neon-lit centerpiece, with art deco hotels on Ocean Beach, the busy streets of Little Cuba and a cruise port that calls itself the capital of the world.
  • Leonida KeysA laid-back island chain of boat parties, dive bars and reef diving. It's also Jason's home turf.
  • GrassriversLeonida's vast wetlands, full of stilt houses, mangroves, alligators and stranger things hidden in the water.
  • Port GellhornLeonida's forgotten coast: a faded resort town of empty strip malls and run-down motels, where the drug trade is filling the gap.
  • AmbrosiaAn industrial town by Lake Leonida, built around the Allied Crystal sugar refinery and run by a local biker gang.
  • Mount Kalaga National ParkThe wild northern border of Leonida, home to hunting, fishing, kayaking, off-road trails and some deeply suspicious locals.

Side quests start at launch

Strangers, odd jobs and side activities will be listed here region by region once the game is out on November 19.
