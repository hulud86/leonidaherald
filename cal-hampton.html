Who's Who

Cal Hampton

Jason's homebody friend, who'd rather sit at home with a beer, eavesdrop on the Coast Guard and read up on conspiracy theories.

By The Leonida Herald, November 17, 2026

Cal is Jason's friend and another member of Brian Heder's circle in the Keys. Where Jason wants something bigger, Cal is happy where he is: at home, with a few beers, listening to Coast Guard radio traffic and browsing the stranger corners of the internet.

What we know

  • Rockstar plays him as the paranoid one, the kind of guy who half-believes everything he reads online.
  • Monitoring Coast Guard communications would be a handy skill for anyone moving goods by boat, which hints at how he might help the crew.
  • His comfortable routine looks likely to be disrupted once Jason and Lucia's plans pick up speed.

Still to come

After launch, we'll note the missions Cal appears in and anything he unlocks, such as information, jobs or equipment.
